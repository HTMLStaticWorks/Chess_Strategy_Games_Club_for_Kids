Step-by-Step Method

How Our Strategy Club Works

From foundational rules to tournament trophies, our proven 4-stage process guides young players to strategic success.

01

Initial Skill Evaluation

Interactive diagnostic match to evaluate current tactical vision and place your child in the right peer group.

02

Weekly Masterclasses

Engaging small-group sessions focused on opening ideas, tactical combinations, and endgame principles.

03

Strategy Board Arena

Weekend open arena with Settlers of Catan Jr, Blokus, and casual chess blitz to build spatial and resource planning.

04

Scholastic Tournaments

Compete in monthly USCF rated tournaments, earn trophies, and track rating gains in the Parent Dashboard.
