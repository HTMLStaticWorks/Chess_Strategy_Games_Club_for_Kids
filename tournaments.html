Tournament Preparation

What to Expect on Tournament Day

Standardized USCF tournament regulations designed to cultivate focus, discipline, and respect.

Touch-Move Rule

If a player touches a piece on their turn, they must move it if it has a legal move available.

Digital Chess Clocks

Matches feature standard G/30 delay-5 clocks to teach time management and composure under pressure.

USCF Rating Reports

All official rated section games are submitted to USCF and reflected directly in the Parent Dashboard.

Trophies & Medals

Custom engraved trophies for top finishers and official achievement ribbons for every participating child.

Pro Arena Gear

Scholastic Competition & Equipment Standards

We equip young players with official USCF FIDE gear to ensure professional tournament readiness.

FIDE Digital Clocks & Delay Timers

Every board features official DGT digital clocks set for 15+0 blitz or 30+5 delay, teaching young players time management under competitive pressure.

USCF Triple-Weighted Sets

Heavyweight Staunton tournament pieces and green vinyl roll-up boards standard across national scholastic championships.

Algebraic Match Scoresheets

Official match notation booklets provided to record moves during games for post-match analysis with FIDE grandmaster coaches.

Scholastic Trophies & Instant Ratings

Trophies and medals awarded to top 5 players per rating tier, with rating results updated directly in the Parent Dashboard within 24 hours.
