Membership Info

Pricing & Enrollment FAQ

Answers to common parent questions about billing, sibling discounts, and session flexibilities.

Do you offer sibling discounts?
Yes! Enrolling a second child grants an automatic 15% monthly discount applied to both memberships directly in your Parent Portal.
Can I change plans or pause during school breaks?
Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or pause your membership anytime from the Settings tab in your Parent Dashboard without penalty.
What happens if my child misses a weekly session?
Missed masterclasses can be made up during any weekend strategy game arena or alternative weekday session within 30 days.
Is there a money-back guarantee for first-timers?
We offer a 14-day full refund guarantee if your child does not love their first masterclass session and arena games.
